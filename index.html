Program Educational Objectives (PEOs)

PEO 1 Practice & Competence

Professional Practice & Technical Competence

Demonstrated professional competence in computer engineering through impactful practice, technical leadership, and innovative design across hardware, software, network infrastructure, and embedded systems.

PEO 2 Ethics & Society

Ethical Leadership & Social Responsibility

Maintained high ethical standards, social responsibility, sustainability consciousness, and safety compliance while providing engineering solutions to complex societal and industrial problems.

PEO 3 Lifelong Learning

Continuous Growth & Adaptive Leadership

Engaged in continuous lifelong learning, professional certifications, advanced graduate studies, and adaptive technical leadership in emerging digital and computing paradigms.
